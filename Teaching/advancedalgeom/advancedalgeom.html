Syllabus

  1. i (Pre-)sheaves; sheaves of modules over topological spaces.
  2. ii Spectrum of a ring; stalks; coordinate rings; dimension.
  3. iii Schemes; gluing; reduced and integral schemes.
  4. iv Products; separated and proper schemes.
  5. v Projective morphisms; flat morphisms; birational maps.
  6. vi Coherent sheaves and vector bundles; divisors and line bundles.
  7. vii Sheaf cohomology; derived push-forward and pull-back; base change.
  8. viii Serre duality theorem; semicontinuity theorems.
  9. ix Functor of points; group schemes; Hilbert schemes.
