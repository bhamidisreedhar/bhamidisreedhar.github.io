Research

I am interested in problems related to algebraic geometry. The questions that I generally spend time thinking about are related to K-theory, Chow groups and matrix factorization categories.

Publications and preprints

  • Localization by 2-periodic complexes and virtual structure sheaves, with Jeongseok Oh ( Journal of the Institute of Mathematics of Jussieu 21, no. 5 (2022): 1477–1506) [Journal] [ArXiv]
  • Virtual equivariant Grothendieck-Riemann-Roch formula, with Charanya Ravi ( Doc. Math., 26, 2061-2094 (2021)) [Journal] [ArXiv]
